Before you connect

  1. Close the Serial Monitor in the Arduino IDE. Only one program can use the COM port.
  2. The first time, press Connect to Arduino and pick the port. After that the app connects by itself.
  3. To upload a sketch from the Arduino IDE, press Disconnect here first (or close the app).
